Cornerstone

How to play

  1. The goal. Fit as many of your squares on the board as you can.
  2. Your first piece must cover the marked corner square of the board for your colour.
  3. Every piece after that must touch one of your own pieces at a corner only. Your own pieces may never touch along a side.
  4. Other colours can touch yours any way they like. They will try to block you!
  5. Stuck? If none of your pieces fit, you pass. The game ends when nobody can place another piece.

Playing a piece

  1. Choose a piece from the list.
  2. Tap or click the board to preview it, or use the ▲ ◀ ▶ ▼ buttons to move it one square at a time. Use Rotate and Flip to turn it.
  3. Press Place piece. You can also tap the previewed piece again.

Scoring

Each square left in your hand is -1 point. Place all 21 pieces for +15. Finish with the single square for +5 more. Highest score wins.

Keyboard

Click the board, then use the arrow keys to move a piece, R to rotate, F to flip, [ and ] to change piece, and Enter to place it.
